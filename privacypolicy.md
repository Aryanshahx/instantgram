# InstantGram Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 October 2026**
**App:** InstantGram (Android, package `com.hypertechlabs.instantgram`)
**Made by:** HyperTech Labs ("we", "us")
**Contact:** techlabs.hyper@gmail.com

This policy explains what information InstantGram collects, why, where it is stored, who can see it, and the choices you have. By using InstantGram you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

---

## 1. Who can use InstantGram

You must be **at least 13 years old** to create an account. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up to check this. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it. If you are a parent and think your child under 13 has an account, email us.

## 2. Information you give us

| Information | Why we need it | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name and username | Your public profile | Everyone on InstantGram |
| Email address | Log in, password reset, important messages | Only you (and us) |
| Password | Log in | Nobody, not even us (stored by Google Firebase as a secure hash) |
| Date of birth | Check you are 13+; age range in post Analytics | **Only you.** Never shown on your profile |
| Language | App language and Analytics totals | Only you (others only see totals) |
| Country | Analytics totals for creators | Only you (others only see totals) |
| Profile photo, cover photo, bio, website | Your public profile | Everyone on InstantGram |
| Posts, clips and moments (photos, videos, music you add, captions, hashtags) | Sharing them is the purpose of the app | The audience you choose (see section 5) |
| Comments, likes, reposts, saves, shares | Interacting with posts | Likes, comments and reposts can be seen by others. **Saves are private** |
| Messages (chats) | Direct messages | Only you and the person you chat with |
| Who you follow, follow requests, blocked accounts | Your feed and privacy | Followers/following lists are visible to others. **Blocked accounts are private** |
| Reports and account-deletion requests | Safety and support | Sent by email to us only |

## 3. Information collected when you use the app

- **Views:** When you view someone else's post or clip, we save **one view record per account** for that post. It contains: whether you follow the creator, your country, your language, your age range (for example "18-24", never your birth date), where you saw it (Discover, Clips, Profile, Explore), how many times you viewed it, how long you watched a video and whether you watched it to the end.
  The creator **only sees totals** in Analytics (for example "40% from India" or "62% followers"). The creator **never sees who viewed** their post.
  Your own views of your own posts are not counted.
- **Notifications:** When someone likes, comments, follows or messages you, a notification is saved to your account.
- **Technical data:** Our providers (Google Firebase, Vercel, Tigris) may automatically log technical information such as IP address, device type and request time to keep the service secure and working. We do not use this for advertising.

## 4. Information kept only on your phone

These stay on your device and are **not uploaded** to us:

- Watch history (Settings → Your activity → History)
- Daily time spent in the app and your daily limit
- Seen moments, mute and auto-scroll settings, accessibility settings
- The **error log** (Settings → About → Error log). It is only sent to us if **you** choose "Email us".

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information.

## 5. Who can see your content

- **Public account:** anyone on InstantGram can see your posts, clips and moments.
- **Private account:** only followers you approve can see them.
- **Per post audience:** each post, clip or moment can be set to **Everyone**, **Followers** or **Only me**.
- **Hidden counts:** you can hide likes, comments, reposts or shares counts on a post.
- **Moments** disappear after **24 hours**. Posts and clips stay until you delete them.
- **Blocked accounts** cannot see your content or comment on your posts.
- Anyone who can see your content can take screenshots or share links. Please think before you post.

## 6. Photos, videos and music

- Photos, videos and music you upload are stored with **Tigris** object storage and are delivered with public web links, so the app can show them. Anyone who has the exact link can open the file.
- Music you add from your phone is uploaded with the post. **Only add music you have the right to use.**
- The editor (crop, filters, text) works on your phone before uploading.

## 7. Phone permissions

- **Photos and videos / storage:** to choose photos, videos and music from your phone. The app only reads the files you pick.
- **Camera** (if you take a photo or video in the app).
- **Internet:** to use the service.
- **Media volume:** the Clips screen can change the phone's media volume when you drag up or down.

We never read files you did not pick, your contacts, your location or your microphone.

## 8. Where your information is stored (our service providers)

| Provider | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) | Accounts, profiles, posts data, comments, messages, follows, views, notifications |
| **Tigris Data** | Storage of photos, videos and music |
| **Vercel** | Our small server that checks your login and creates secure upload links |
| **GitHub** | Hosts app updates. The "App update" screen asks GitHub for the newest version number |

These providers may store data in data centres outside your country, including the United States. They process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

## 9. What we do NOT do

- We do **not sell** your personal information.
- We do **not show ads** and do not share your data with advertisers.
- We do **not** use your private messages, date of birth or saved posts for anything other than running the app.

## 10. How long we keep information

- Your account and content stay until **you delete** them or ask us to delete your account.
- Moments are hidden after 24 hours.
- When you delete a post, its photos, videos and music are removed from storage.
- After an account-deletion request, we delete your account and content **within 30 days**. Copies in provider backups may take a short extra time to disappear.

## 11. Your choices and rights

You can:

- Edit your profile, language and country (Settings → Account, Settings → Language)
- Make your account private (Settings → Account privacy)
- Choose the audience of each post, hide counts, delete posts and moments
- Block people and approve or decline follow requests
- Clear your watch history on this phone
- **Delete your account:** go to Settings → Delete account, which sends us an email. You cannot delete the account directly in the app. We confirm by email and delete it within 30 days.
- Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by emailing techlabs.hyper@gmail.com

Depending on where you live (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR in Europe), you may have extra rights. Email us and we will help.

## 12. Security

We use Firebase security rules so that, for example, only you can edit your profile and only chat members can read a chat. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. No system is 100% secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will change. Big changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
