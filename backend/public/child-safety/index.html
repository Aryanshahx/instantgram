InstantGram

Child Safety Standards

Standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) · Hyper Tech Labs · Last updated: 16 October 2026
Zero tolerance. InstantGram does not allow any content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Accounts that break these rules are removed and reported to the authorities.

1. Who can use InstantGram

  • You must be at least 13 years old. Every new account must enter a date of birth when signing up, and accounts under 13 can not be created.
  • For people under 18, sensitive content is always hidden and this can not be turned off.

2. What is not allowed

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated material.
  • Sexualising minors in posts, clips, moments, comments, captions, profiles or messages.
  • Grooming: contacting or befriending a minor to sexually abuse or exploit them.
  • Sextortion: threatening a minor or demanding sexual images, money or favours.
  • Child trafficking, or offering or asking for sexual contact with minors.
  • Sharing, asking for or linking to any of the above, including links to other websites.

3. How we prevent it

  • Automatic checks: every new post, clip, moment and comment is checked automatically by our moderation system (Hyper Intelligence). Content that may involve minors is hidden right away and marked urgent for human review.
  • Human review: our moderators review flagged and reported content. Child safety cases are always handled first.
  • Strikes and blocking: people who break the rules get strikes, can be stopped from posting, or are blocked permanently. Child safety violations lead to an immediate, permanent block.

4. How to report in the app

  • Tap ⋯ on any post, clip, moment, comment or profile → Report → choose Child safety.
  • Content reported for child safety is hidden at once, before review, and goes to the top of our review list.
  • You can also email us at techlabs.hyper@gmail.com.

5. What we do when we find CSAE

  • We remove the content and permanently block the account.
  • We report it to the relevant authorities, including India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), and to other national or regional authorities where the law requires it.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests that follow the law.

6. Laws we follow

We follow all child safety laws that apply to us, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act 2012, the Information Technology Act 2000 (section 67B) and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021, as well as Google Play's child safety standards policy.

7. If a child is in danger

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the local police first. In India call 112 (emergency) or 1098 (Childline). You can also report online at cybercrime.gov.in.
