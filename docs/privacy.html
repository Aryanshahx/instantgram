Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

InstantGram ("the app", "we") is a place to share photos, clips and moments. This page explains what we keep, why, and the choices you have. By making an account you accept this policy.

What we keep

  • Account details: name, username, email address, date of birth, country, and language.
  • What you post: photos, clips, moments, captions, comments, messages and voice notes, plus simple counts such as likes, views and reposts.
  • Lists connected to you: who you follow, people you blocked, saved posts and follow requests.
  • Your location only if you choose to add a place to a post. It is never collected in the background.
  • On your phone only: watch history, screen time, accessibility and language settings.

Why we keep it

To run the app: to show your content to the people you choose, to let people find and message you, to make calls work, to keep the service safe, and to show you content in your language and region. We do not sell your personal data.

Who can see it

Posts are visible to everyone unless you pick Followers only or Only me, or switch on a private account. Messages are visible to the people in the chat. Your username, name and photo are visible to other users. Your date of birth and email are not shown to other users.

Where it is stored

Account data, comments and messages are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Photos, clips and voice files are stored in our own object storage. Music comes from Epidemic Sound and GIFs and stickers from Giphy; the app asks those services for content when you search for it. Voice and video calls use Agora.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete your posts and comments at any time.
  • Hide like and view counts, make your account private, block people, and clear your history in Settings.
  • Ask us to delete your account and its data by writing to the address below. We will remove it within a reasonable time.

Children

You must be at least 13 years old to make an account. That is why we ask for your date of birth. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we remove it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and access to stored data is limited by rules that only let you change your own information. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Using the app after a change means you accept the new version.
